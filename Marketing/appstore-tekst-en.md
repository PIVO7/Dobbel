## Description (max 4000 tekens)

> Dobbel! is the classic five-dice game, made for kids and the whole family. Roll up to three times, hold your best dice and pick the smartest box on the scorecard. Who counts the cleverest?
>
> TOGETHER ON ONE DEVICE
> Play against each other on a single iPhone or iPad. The device goes from hand to hand, with a friendly pass screen so nobody peeks.
>
> OR AGAINST THE COMPUTER
> Three opponents, each with their own character: Snoozy just rolls anything, Robbie puts up a decent fight and Professor Dot is dead serious. So the game grows along with your child.
>
> LEARNING WITHOUT LESSONS
> A built-in tip shows which box is the smart choice — and explains why, if you ask. New players get a short tour, and the rules sheet "How does Dobbel work?" is never far away. Meanwhile plenty of adding and multiplying is going on, but we just call it playing.
>
> MADE FOR LITTLE HANDS
> Big buttons, cheerful colors, a personal avatar for every player, and dice you can roll by shaking the device. Every game ends with confetti for the winner.
>
> SAFE AND CALM
> No ads, no accounts, no internet required. Everything stays on your own device. Period.
>
> START FREE, UNLOCK ONCE
> For free you play with two players, against Robbie, in the classic theme. The one-time Family Version unlocks games with 3 or 4 players, all three opponents, the Candy, Ocean and Night color themes, and per-player statistics — with records and win streaks. One purchase for the whole family, through Family Sharing. And it sits behind a parental gate, so little fingers can't buy anything by accident.
>
> Grab the dice. Dobbel!
